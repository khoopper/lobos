## Deviations from the live page

Two, both recorded deliberately. Everything else is a 1:1 emulation.

### 1. The "Fotografías de la semana" gallery is broken in production

**What the original does:** the Elementor justified-gallery widget (`e5ab0c6`) renders 11
`<a class="e-gallery-item">` elements carrying `data-thumbnail`, `data-width` and `data-height`,
but Elementor's `e-gallery` layout script never runs. Every `.e-gallery-image` stays 0px tall and
the whole section collapses to **21px**.

**Verification:** confirmed at 1440, 768 and 390px with a real input-event scroll plus an
IntersectionObserver trigger and a 3.5s settle. In every case `firstItemRect.height === 0` and
`getComputedStyle(.e-gallery-image).backgroundImage === "none"`.

**What the clone does:** renders the gallery the way the widget is configured — justified rows from
the 11 real thumbnails at their real intrinsic sizes, `ideal_row_height` 300px desktop / 150px
tablet and mobile, 10px gap, dark overlay fading in on hover, each item linking to the full-size
original (`link_to: "file"`).

**Effect on measurements:** the gallery section is 920px tall at 1440 and 1270px at 390, against
21px on the original. This is the single largest difference in total page height.

**To match the original exactly instead**, render nothing between the "Fotografías de la semana"
heading and the footer.

### 2. The Google-reviews widget is rebuilt rather than embedded

**What the original does:** an Elementor shortcode widget injects the **Trustindex** Google-reviews
slider from `cdn.trustindex.io` (`data-layout-id="5"`, `data-layout-category="slider"`,
`ti-col-4`, `data-pager-autoplay-timeout="6"`). Because it loads asynchronously the section
measures 20px until the script resolves, then expands to ~271px.

**What the clone does:** reproduces the rendered output — the "EXCELENTE" rating block, five stars,
"A base de 2976 reseñas", and the six real reviews captured from the live DOM — as a local
component with the same 6s autoplay and the same "Leer más" clamp. The third-party script is not
embedded, because it is bound to the site owner's own Trustindex account.

**Effect on measurements:** 294px against the original's 271px loaded state (+23px at 1440).
